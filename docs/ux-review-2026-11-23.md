## 1. Summary

The import → detect → fill → sign → send path works on both platforms, and the three build-24 tester issues are gone where a simulator can show them. Field detection on the demo form is strong (13 fields, correct labels and types, `iOSf/02_detect.png`), and PreFill fills matching fields with one tap and gives Undo (`iOS/11_w4_prefilled.png`). The single biggest bottleneck is the last step: the action the user came for ("Flatten & Sign (locks the document)") is a red, outlined, "danger" button under a filled "Save Draft" button, and it opens a warning dialog in jargon ("Flattening bakes your entries…"). Accessibility is the second problem: on the fill screen every field is announced only as "Tap to fill…", checkboxes have no name, and most secondary text measures 1.8–2.6:1 contrast.

Happy-path step count (measured from `integration_test/store_capture_test.dart` and the Android variant run):

- Import (1) → system picker (1–3) → Detect Fields (1) → Fill 13 fields (1) → per text field: tap + type + Save (2 taps each) → checkbox (1) → signature box (1) → draw → Use This Signature (1) → Review & Finish (1) → Flatten & Sign (1) → Flatten & Lock (1) → Share PDF (1) → target.
- Minimum with no typing: 12 taps. Store flow with one PreFill: 14 taps plus typing of 2 fields.
- Time for a 2-page form: estimated 2–3 minutes for a first-time user who types 8 fields. Not measured on a person.

## 4. Top 3 quick wins (under half a day each)

1. Finish button hierarchy and copy (#1): make the finish action the filled primary button, "Save Draft" secondary, plain words in all 7 languages.
2. Contrast tokens (#3): replace `#9E9E9E`, `#FFA000`, `#FFB300` text uses with theme tokens that pass 4.5:1; dark text on the Draft badge.
3. Accessible names in fill and detect modes (#2): semantics label = field label + value/state; checkboxes get their printed label.

## 5. Top 3 structural changes

1. One fill screen instead of Detect → Fill (2–3 days). Open the document straight in fill mode after detection. Put field editing behind "Edit Fields" (already there). Remove "Confirm All", "Skip to Fill" and the second PreFill button (#5, #6). Saves 1–2 taps and one mode switch on every document.
2. "Add signature" when none is found (2–3 days). In fill mode, if no Sign field exists, show a bar that places a Sign field (and a Date field) where the user taps. Add Sign/Date boxes from printed labels on AcroForm forms such as the W-4 (#4).
3. Simpler Library card and filters (2 days). Whole card opens; actions in a "⋯" menu; one filter row; card text wraps at word breaks and works at font 1.3 and in Tamil (#9, #10, #13, #14).

## 8. Assumptions

- The missing back button on `iOS/24_detect_picture_no_fields.png` and `iOS/25_scan_review_save_pdf.png` comes from `router.go` in the test. Real users reach these screens by push. Not reported.
- "0p" on the Receipt card (`iOS/28_library_categories.png`) comes from a test-built document that sets no page count. Not reported.
- The skewed page in `iOS/15_photo_cropped_review.png` comes from a synthetic test photo. Not reported.
- iOS card buttons and field boxes have the same sizes as on Android (same Flutter layout). iOS sizes are estimated, not measured.
- Contrast values take the most common colour as background and the most distant pixel as text. Anti-aliasing can only lower the true value.
- The working tree changed during this review (`analysis_options.yaml`, `scripts/release.sh`, `tool/verify/device_check.sh`, new upgrade-verify files). Another agent made these changes. The device runs in this review started before them.
