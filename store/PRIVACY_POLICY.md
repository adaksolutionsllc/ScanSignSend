# Privacy Policy — Scan Sign Send

**Effective date:** [FILL IN before publishing]
**Publisher:** ADAK Ventures

## The short version
Scan Sign Send does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data. Everything you scan, type, and sign stays on your device. We have no servers and no accounts.

## What we access (and why)
- **Camera** — to scan paper documents. Images are captured and processed only on your device.
- **Photos / files** — only the images or PDFs you explicitly choose to import. They stay on your device.
- **Face ID / fingerprint** — optional, used only to unlock the app locally. We never receive your biometric data; it is handled entirely by your device's operating system.

## What we collect
Nothing. The app has no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no crash-reporting that leaves the device, and no network calls for your document data.

## Purchases
The one-time "Full Access" purchase is processed by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment information. Purchase validation is handled by the platform's in-app purchase system.

## Data storage & deletion
Your documents, signatures, and profile details are stored in a private database on your device. Deleting the app removes all of this data. We cannot access or recover it because we never receive it.

## Children
The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the Privacy Policy URL listed in the app stores.
