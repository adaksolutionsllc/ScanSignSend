## Full description

**Scan Sign Send turns paper into signed PDFs — entirely on your device.**

Scan any document with your camera, tap to fill in fields, add your signature with your finger, and share the finished PDF. There are no accounts, no subscriptions, and nothing is ever uploaded to a server. Your documents stay private on your phone.

**Scan**
• High-quality document scanning with automatic edge detection
• Multi-page batches — up to 20 pages per scan
• Reorder, rotate, and clean up pages before you sign
• Import existing PDFs and images too

**Sign & Fill**
• Automatic field detection finds text, date, checkbox, and signature spots
• Drag and resize any field to fit your form exactly
• Draw your signature once and reuse it across documents
• Smart autofill for your name, email, phone, and address

**Send**
• Flatten everything into a clean, final PDF with a signing certificate page
• Share via Mail, Messages, AirDrop, or any app
• Reusable templates are saved automatically so you never re-scan the same form

**Private by design**
• 100% offline — no internet permission needed to do the work
• Optional Face ID / fingerprint lock
• One-time purchase. Own it forever. No subscription.

Built by ADAK Ventures.
