# Google Play phone screenshots, September 2026 (retired)

The six English (en-US) phone screenshots the Play listing carried until
13 October 2026, in Play's order, downloaded from Play before
`fastlane play_screenshots` replaced them with the localized set from
`tool/store_capture` (capture_android.sh + frame.py).

They were made by hand (1080×1920) and show 1.0-era screens: the old fill
chip bar ("Today"), no PreFill. Kept for reference only; nothing reads them.
