# Scan Sign Send

Scan any paper document, fill it in, sign it, and share a finished PDF — all on your phone.

## Offline and private
No account, no server, no subscription. Everything you scan, type and sign stays on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

## What it does
- Scan pages with your camera, or import a PDF or photo
- Finds the blanks on your form automatically — lines, gaps, signature and date spots — and learns from your corrections
- Fill text, dates, checkboxes, radio buttons, initials and signatures
- Share a flattened, signed PDF, or a fillable PDF others can complete
- Works in English, Français, Español, Português, हिन्दी, தமிழ், తెలుగు

## Pricing
Free to try: finish 2 documents of up to 2 pages each. Full Access is a one-time purchase for unlimited documents of any length.
